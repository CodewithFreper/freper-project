Intermediate Vocational Training in Pharmacy

What I Studied:


A vocational program covering medication dispensing, stock management, administrative tasks, and patient counseling.



Skills & Knowledge Gained:

  • Inventory Management Systems: Learned to use pharmacy management software to track stock, manage expirations, and process orders—building familiarity with business-critical systems.
  • Process Adherence: Followed strict protocols for dispensing medication, ensuring accuracy and patient safety—a mindset that translates to following ITIL or other IT service management frameworks.
  • Administrative Management: Developed skills in handling administrative tasks, including record-keeping, billing, and documentation, which are essential for creating support tickets and maintaining system logs.
  • Customer Service Excellence: Trained to handle sensitive patient information with care, provide clear instructions, and manage expectations—key skills for a helpdesk environment.
  • Multitasking: Managed simultaneous responsibilities: assisting patients, managing stock, and maintaining records, preparing me for the fast-paced IT support environment where priorities shift rapidly.

This program taught me how to work in a high-stakes, compliance-driven environment where accuracy and customer focus are paramount.
I learned to:

  • Manage inventory and data in real-time systems.
  • Follow procedures strictly while handling complex situations.
  • Communicate clearly and empathetically with users.
  • Balance multiple tasks without compromising quality.
